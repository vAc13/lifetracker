Lifetracker privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Lifetracker is a personal tracker used by its owner. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share any data.

What the app accesses

Google Drive: the app creates and edits one file, Lifetracker/lifetracker-data.json, in your own Drive. It can only see files it created itself.

Google Calendar: the app lists your calendars and their events so it can show them, and adds or deletes events only when you ask it to.

Your email address is used only to speed up signing in again.

Where data is stored

Your entries are stored in your Google Drive and cached in your browser on your own device so the app works offline. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Removing access

Sign out in the app's settings, or remove Lifetracker at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Drive file removes your data.
